# Annotating metaphor in Czech poetry

Our team consists of: Barbora Štěpánková, Karel Vlk and Jan Pijálek.

We annotated 591 words from 6 poems from Jiří Dědeček and František Gellner. In this text, we found 124, 120 and 141 metaphors respectively. 

## Instructions

We followed the annotation instructions of a paper which modified the MIPVU procedure to Czech language (https://anndh18.github.io/papers/pavlas.pdf). We inserted a <met> marker after every metaphor in the text. The procedure is not well adapted to poetry and multi-word metaphors.

## Inter-annotator agreement

Kappa of 0.2 - 0.4 is fair agreement, 0.4 - 0.6 is moderate agreement and 0.6 - 0.8 is substantial agreement. 

### Cohen's kappa

We computed pairwise Cohen's kappa between each two pairs of annotators and the results are as follows:

- Bara, Karel: 0.45
- Bara, Honza: 0.38
- Karel, Honza: 0.73

### Fleiss' kappa

The Fleiss' kappa of all labels is 0.53, which is considered a moderate agreement.
